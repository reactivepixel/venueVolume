## Search limitations and data gaps

- The acquisition JSON is intentionally conservative: unknown specs remain null or image lists empty, and acquisition errors name the gap. In particular, no DMX behavior is inferred from neighboring model numbers.
- Search results and official pages exposed numerous additional candidate names but many require opening each product page, resolving a precise variant/SKU, collecting its official still-image URL and reading a manual/dimensional drawing. Exact manufacturer page links should be resolved before promoting the discovery-only rows below.
- Blizzard pages offer rich indexed specifications but their searched excerpts did not surface stable direct image links or complete dimensioned drawings for all candidates.
- CHAUVET DJ download pages gate some manuals behind an email form, though product-page specs and official image URLs remain accessible.
- Eurolite/Steinigke product pages often publish a DMX profile but not clear assembled dimensions in page text; manual and drawing download review is still needed.
- SHEHDS product pages sometimes have inconsistent electrical fields and unlabeled size tuples, as recorded per item. These remain research candidates, not validated hardware profiles.
- Showtec's current product index was not found through the official search results; Highlite manufacturer manuals provided solid model-level evidence for Phantom 65 and Compact Par 7 Tri.
