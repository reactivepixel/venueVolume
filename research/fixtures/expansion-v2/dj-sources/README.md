## Source quality notes

Primary product pages/manuals are classified as manufacturer sources in the JSON. Steinigke is the Eurolite brand owner's official storefront and product-download host. Search-engine result excerpts were used for candidate discovery, then the cited official pages/manuals were opened for core facts where possible. A product name discovered from a category listing is not considered sufficient by itself to claim control capability.
