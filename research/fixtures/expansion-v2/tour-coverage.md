## Records in `tour.json`

54 model records total. Six ROBE product-page records include exact official gallery images, dimensions, power, weight, and DMX/RDM compatibility. Martin includes MAC One Beam, both Encore Performance variants, both Encore Wash variants, Aura PXL/Raven XIP/XB, Encore Two and Viper XIP; the final pair now have official assembled dimensions. Ayrton source-complete records include Rivale Profile/Wash, Stradale Profile, Karif LT, Mistral S/TC, Diablo Profile S/TC, Levante S/TC, Bora S/TC, Eurus Profile S/TC, Rawbeam 350, Khamsin S/TC, Cobra², Perseo Beam, Perseo Profile S/TC, Veloce Profile, Veloce Wash, Argo 6 FX/Wash, Zonda 3 FX/Wash and Nando 502 Wash (the latter retains a conflicting dimension-height revision note). One record each adds Vari-Lite VL10 BeamWash and GLP MAD MAXX with exact official image URLs and physical specifications. One record retains an explicit acquisition error: Volero Twins' dual-engine/endless-pan movement geometry is unsupported. Arolla Aqua LT/HP and Aqua Wash HC retain exact discovery rows with acquisition errors where the required exact-model image/specification captures remain incomplete. B-Eye 7, B-Eye K30, Rivale Profile, Volero Cube/Twins and MAD MAXX have nonstandard motion or geometry limitations called out. Ayrton index coverage remains broader than the source-complete subset.

Every record uses official manufacturer URLs. 51 of 54 have exact official images and all three dimensions populated; the three incomplete Arolla variants are explicitly marked with blockers. The ROBE rows have verified DMX512/RDM control compatibility; exact mode footprints remain unknown except Tetra1. The four Encore rows specify DMX512-A/RDM and 38-channel Performance or 24-channel Wash footprints. The newer Claypaky B-Eye 7/K30 and Arolla Aqua/Cube records include official control compatibility and mode channel counts. Empty images and `acquisition_errors` remain a hold on acquisition/build only where named. The full six-brand inventory is not complete, particularly Ayrton archive/current range and ROBE's dynamic exact-item index.

## Evidence and caveats

- Martin's MAC family page provided exact current model links and its moving-head overview names significant legacy models.
- Claypaky's official product index provided exact new product names and model codes; product pages were opened for the cited Volero Cube, Volero Twins, B-Eye 7 and Arolla Aqua records.
- Ayrton's official index and product pages support exact source-backed records including Cobra², Khamsin S/TC, Perseo Beam, Perseo Profile S/TC and Veloce Profile. The S/TC product codes, imagery, dimensions, power, controls and channel ranges are retained separately where Ayrton exposes distinct ordering codes.
- Martin's Encore product pages list DMX compliance to USITT DMX512-A, RDM compliance to ANSI/ESTA E1.20, and the 38-channel Performance / 24-channel Wash modes. Encore Two and Viper XIP physical envelopes were transcribed from official product-page dimension specifications.
- Ayrton official product pages supplied full physical/control/source records for Karif LT, Diablo Profile S/TC, Levante S/TC and Zonda 3 Wash; dual S/TC product variants remain separate product-code rows.
- Additional Ayrton page/specification research established Mistral S/TC, Eurus Profile S/TC and Rawbeam 350; codes, dimensions, controls and official exact-model image URLs were transcribed.
- Ayrton manufacturer pages now fully support Bora S/TC, Veloce Wash, and Argo 6 FX/Wash, including dimensions, DMX/network protocols, physical source counts and control footprints.
- Claypaky official pages expose DMX/RDM/network control plus distinct main/pixel/strobe channel footprints for Volero Cube, B-Eye 7/K30 and Arolla Aqua. Cube's unusual square-optic geometry is represented by the application's custom geometry support; Volero Twins remains blocked by unsupported dual-engine/endless-pan movement.
- GLP and Vari-Lite indexes establish product-family coverage but the current catalog already contains many top touring models. Do not import existing IDs a second time.
- ROBE's products page is a dynamic application. Plain HTML reveals series and the product collection is rendered from embedded data; the full item-by-item inventory was not conclusively extracted here.
- The existing `research/fixtures/expansion/tour.json` contains prior batch records. Those entries are not copied into this batch; matching IDs in `assets/fixtures/catalog.json` remain excluded.
