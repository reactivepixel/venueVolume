# DJ fixture visual/runtime QA

Review date: 2026-10-02. This was a read-only review of currently built assets in the parent `catalog-expansion-v2` worktree. The machine-readable findings are in [dj-qa.json](dj-qa.json).

The earlier review covered 15 built fixtures; in this final pass I rechecked Focus Spot 6Z, Phantom 100 Spot and KLS-120 against their stored official reference images and previews, and added Intimidator Spot Duo. I also checked the generated runtime sidecars for Duo and KLS-120. Duo has four separate pan/tilt joints and two emitters; KLS-120 shows four cans mounted beneath the bar and has four emitters with manual aiming pivots. The Duo's dual-head arrangement and KLS-120's four-can arrangement match their references. The runtime sidecar/native validation status is recorded by the parent build; this visual QA did not constitute a device or headset test.

## Findings

- **ADJ Focus Spot 6Z — medium:** [source image](../../../assets/fixtures/adj/focus-spot-6z/sources/product-reference-0.jpg) vs. [three-quarter render](../../../assets/fixtures/adj/focus-spot-6z/previews/three-quarter.png). Its long, tapered/faceted optical housing is represented by a short rounded generic shell. Preserve its documented envelope but reshape the housing to match the source silhouette.
- **Showtec Phantom 100 Spot — medium:** [source image](../../../assets/fixtures/showtec/phantom-100-spot/sources/product-reference-0.png) vs. [three-quarter render](../../../assets/fixtures/showtec/phantom-100-spot/previews/three-quarter.png). The source's short angular/tapered head with small lens hood appears as a smooth sphere with an oversized planar ring. Replace the head primitive with a compact faceted/tapered form within the existing envelope.

The [Intimidator Spot Duo reference](../../../assets/fixtures/chauvet-dj/intimidator-spot-duo/sources/product-reference-0.jpg) and [render](../../../assets/fixtures/chauvet-dj/intimidator-spot-duo/previews/three-quarter.png) both show two distinct movers on one crossbar. Its generated [runtime rig](../../../assets/fixtures/chauvet-dj/intimidator-spot-duo/models/rig.json) carries separate pan/tilt chains and emitters for both heads, so no articulation or identity issue was found. Likewise, the [KLS-120 reference](../../../assets/fixtures/eurolite/led-kls-120-compact-light-set/sources/product-reference-0.webp) and [render](../../../assets/fixtures/eurolite/led-kls-120-compact-light-set/previews/three-quarter.png) show the correct four spot cans beneath one control bar.

The other reviewed assets had no additional material identity, scale, or moving-part-family error apparent at the requested QA threshold. This is a source-image/render comparison, not a physical measurement, DMX bench test, or RealityKit/headset test. Entries still in research/blocked state were not treated as built assets and were not visually audited.
