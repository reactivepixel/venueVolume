# Legacy and effects expansion — reviewed coverage

This batch adds 64 distinct inventory rows: 18 legacy lights, 21 atmosphere machines and 25 laser/other effects products. The three source files retain official URLs, technical evidence, images and unresolved facts. Names are deduplicated against the prior catalog: existing Sharpy and MAGIC FX CO2 JET II were not added again.

32 rows produced Blender, USDZ, four previews and rig companions. The other 32 remain research-only with no fabricated asset paths. The complete published CSV contains 323 rows, not a claim of complete manufacturer coverage. Use its `pipelineErrors` and the batch ledger for the current row-level result.

The older-light group includes MAC 250/600/700/2000 variants, Robe ColorSpot/ColorWash AT and Claypaky Alpha 1500 variants. Five older light models have packages: MAC 2000 Profile/Wash and Robe ColorSpot 250 AT, ColorWash 250 AT and ColorWash 575 AT. Several other official archive pages expose facts but no retrievable exact product still, and MAC 700 Wash/2000 Performance lack complete dimensions in the reviewed sources. Optional missing wattage/channel counts alone do not block modeling.

Manufacturer galleries require manual image review: an effect-pattern image is not a product still, an accessory/end detail is not a whole machine, and projected beams are not visible aperture counts. Parent review replaced the Scorpion Dual RGB pattern image, selected the full MDG APS machine and requested uncropped versions of the exact Robe CDN assets. The remaining Le Maitre operating shots and approximate shells are explicitly not visually signed off.

The new equipment generator distinguishes silver fog/snow bodies, road-case hazers, six bubble wheels, sealed cabinets, small foggers and shared-window laser housings. Existing published generator files are preserved. Nine catalog models retain visual correction flags (seven from this batch); all 252 packages require native Mac validation for this release. The hierarchy still has seven unrepresented leaf categories.

Control metadata distinguishes DMX/network capability from documented manual/dedicated/external control. Commercial pyro, laser and atmospheric equipment are visual inventory: no hardware commands, firing maps, emission patterns, exposure calculations or safety bypasses are implemented.
