# Lighting taxonomy source references

This directory contains locally acquired manufacturer and clearly identified secondary product images, specification PDFs, and rendered PDF pages supporting `../lighting-candidates.json`. Candidate `images` arrays use direct image-file URLs only; PDF-derived references have no webpage URL in `images` and instead identify the local raster via `reference_image_file` and `reference_image_locator`.

Direct product images were downloaded and visually reviewed for their exact models, including manufacturer images for Roxie2, Lutin 306LPC, ColorSource CYC Floor, STRIKE 4, UV Flood 36, Pinspot LED II, LED Pixel Tube 360, PIXEL TAPE 16IP, Dynasty Scan, MBMHD3 motor, M-2020 mirror ball, PIXEL DRIVER 170, Rosco 78042 gobo, R3008 Tough Frost, and DS-1000RGB MK5. The Georgia Stage Prestige Velour border-curtain photo is a locally reviewed secondary product image; its thickness is explicitly estimated. Astera NYX Bulb dimensions are from an authorized distributor (secondary evidence); the local photo is from that distributor. ETC accessory sheet pages and City Theatrical's labeled barn-door measurement diagram are local references; the existing lens tube/iris records remain unscaled where their sources omit outer dimensions. The barn-door candidate was not added because the diagram and table do not fully establish all outer extents in a defined open/closed pose. Altman PAR64-AL and Scoop 153 raster references are rendered from cited manufacturer PDFs. PDFs for Lutin/Roxie2 preserve variant-specific dimensional evidence.

Image reuse/distribution rights were not established. Treat local copies as research references only; do not package them in distributed runtime assets without permission review.
