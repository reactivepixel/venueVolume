# Lighting taxonomy source references

This directory contains locally acquired manufacturer product images and specification PDFs supporting `../lighting-candidates.json`. The candidate `images` arrays use direct image-file URLs only; two PDF-derived geometry references have no `images` URL and instead identify their local raster via `reference_image_file` and `reference_image_locator`.

Direct manufacturer product images were downloaded and visually reviewed with their matching exact models: Roxie2, Lutin 306LPC, ColorSource CYC Floor, STRIKE 4, UV Flood 36, Pinspot LED II, LED Pixel Tube 360, PIXEL TAPE 16IP, Dynasty Scan, MBMHD3 motor, and DS-1000RGB MK5. The Showgear S14 E324031 bulb photo is from a retailer page and is likewise locally reviewed. Altman PAR64-AL and Scoop 153 raster references are rendered from the cited manufacturer PDFs; those drawings/photos were visually reviewed. PDFs for Lutin/Roxie2 preserve variant-specific dimensional evidence.

Image reuse/distribution rights were not established. Treat local copies as research references only; do not package them in distributed runtime assets without permission review.
