# Ways of Working

## Principles

- Frame work as a question, a hypothesis, or a deliverable.
- Prefer the smallest experiment that changes a decision.
- Record raw observations separately from conclusions.
- Put durable knowledge in this vault, not only in chat, issues, or memory.
- Keep decisions reversible when uncertainty is high.
- Automate a check after a failure mode is understood.

## Definition of a useful experiment

An experiment states:

1. The uncertainty being reduced.
2. A falsifiable hypothesis or comparison.
3. The method, equipment, and success threshold.
4. The observed result and artifacts.
5. The conclusion, limitations, and next decision.

Use [[../99 Templates/Experiment|Experiment]].

## Decision practice

Create a decision record when a choice:

- affects multiple components or future contributors;
- is costly to reverse;
- resolves a recurring disagreement; or
- constrains formats, protocols, platforms, dependencies, or safety behavior.

Use [[../99 Templates/Decision Record|Decision Record]] and add it to [[../06 Decisions/Decision Index|Decision Index]].

## Documentation lifecycle

Suggested statuses are `draft`, `hypothesis`, `active`, `accepted`, `superseded`, and `archived`. Every canonical note should name an owner and an updated date in frontmatter.
