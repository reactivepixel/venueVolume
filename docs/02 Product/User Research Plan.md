# User Research Plan

## Objective

Identify a narrow initial user segment, its highest-cost lighting workflow, and the standard by which a new controller would be trusted in live use.

## Research questions

- What equipment, software, and control surfaces are used today?
- Which part of setup, programming, operation, or teardown consumes the most effort?
- What mistakes are common, and what is their consequence during a live event?
- Which workflows must work without a network or internet connection?
- How are fixture definitions obtained, corrected, and shared?
- What makes an operator trust or reject a controller?
- What would make switching tools too costly?
- Which inputs and outputs are essential on day one?

## Interview targets

Start with 5–8 people in each plausible early segment. Do not combine findings across segments until their workflows and buying behavior are shown to be similar.

## Evidence to capture

- Direct observations and concise quotes
- Current workflow and tool chain
- Frequency, duration, and consequence of each problem
- Workarounds and switching constraints
- Hardware inventory and representative show scale
- Who chooses, buys, configures, and operates the system
