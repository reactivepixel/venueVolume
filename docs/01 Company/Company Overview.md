# Company Overview

## Purpose

Build lighting-control tools that let people create and operate compelling live environments without fighting the control system.

## Stage

Pre-product R&D. The immediate objective is to reduce product and technical uncertainty, not to scale an organization or infrastructure prematurely.

## Company hypotheses

- A meaningful group of lighting operators is underserved by the tradeoff between basic controller apps and complex professional consoles.
- A modern software workflow can reduce setup time without sacrificing live-operation reliability.
- Good fixture data, clear mental models, and safe defaults can make advanced control more accessible.
- Commodity computers and standard network or USB interfaces can provide acceptable output reliability for an initial market segment.

## Open strategic questions

- Who is the first narrow customer: small venues, touring acts, houses of worship, event producers, installers, or another segment?
- Is the wedge live busking, programmed shows, installation control, or previsualization?
- Which hardware and operating systems must the first product support?
- What should be proprietary, open, or based on existing standards and libraries?
- What evidence would justify moving from R&D to product development?
